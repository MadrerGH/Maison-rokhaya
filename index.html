Our story

Two crafts.
One woman.
Zero fixed address.

Rokhaya brings two passions into one micro-enterprise, rooted in the MMU Melaka community. She braids hair with patience and precision — and flips crêpes with the same dedication. The name? A little twist on the French word crêpé, which means both "crinkled" (as in your natural texture) and, of course, crêpe.

No salon walls. No fixed kitchen. Just real skill, real flavours, and real confidence — delivered to your door or your campus corner.
