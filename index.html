Our story

Two crafts.
One woman.
Zero fixed address.

Rokhaya brings two passions into one enterprise at the heart of the MMU Melaka community. She braids hair with patience and precision — and flips crêpes with the same dedication. The name? A riff on the French word crêpé — meaning both "crinkled" (your natural texture) and, of course, crêpe.

No salon walls. No fixed kitchen. Just real skill, real flavours, and real confidence — delivered wherever you are.
